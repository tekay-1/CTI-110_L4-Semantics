Food

I have a sweet tooth, and Paris being famous for its pastries has been on my travel list for a while.

Aside from just pastries, Paris is the home to many famous cuisine of the French, including:

  • Croissants
  • Pain au Chocolat
  • Baguettes
  • French Onion Soup
  • Steak Frites

History

Paris was once a tiny Celtic fishing village. It grew into a powerful Roman city named Lutetia. Over the next 2000 years, it became France's capital, went through wars and revolutions, and was completely redesigned in the 1800s with its famous wide boulevards to become the global center of art and fashion we know today.


Rough timeline of Paris:

  1. Celtic settlement
  2. Roman city of Lutetia
  3. Capital of France
  4. 19th century redesign
Paris also homes a strong and vibrant football/soccer culture, being the home to one of the biggest clubs of world football, Paris Saint-Germain F.C.
