History

Paris was once a tiny Celtic fishing village. It grew into a powerful Roman city named Lutetia. Over the ntext 2000 years, it became France's capital, went through wars and revolutions, and was completely redesigned in the 1800s with its famous wide boulevards to become the global center of art and fashion we know today.

Paris also homes a strong and vibrant football/soccer culture, being the home to one of the biggest clubs of worl football, Paris Saint-Germain F.C.
